MIRACLE CEO - COLOUR PALETTE PROPOSAL (v3, for approval)

WHAT COMPARABLE APPS USE (brand colours pulled from each site, Oct 2026)

Parenting / child development
- Kinedu: bright sky blue #0086D8, deep navy text #1A1D2E, warm off-white #FBFAF8.
- Huckleberry: cyan #36D2EA, slate #4A566F, white.
- Lovevery: ultramarine #202EA8, pale mint #DFF5F3, grey text.

Gamified / kids
- Duolingo: green #58CC02 plus sky blue #1CB0F6. Loud and high energy.
- Lingokids: orange #FF9500, cream #F8F2E9, deep purple text #301C59.

Calm-but-engaging reference
- Headspace: blue #0061EF, warm off-white #F9F4F2, near-black #2D2C2B.

Patterns worth copying
1. A warm off-white page, never pure white or grey. Cards are white on top of it.
2. One confident, saturated main colour that does the "tap me" job.
3. Dark text tinted toward navy or purple, not pure black. It feels friendlier.
4. Engaging apps add one hot colour (orange or green) for progress and reward. That moment of colour is what makes coming back feel rewarding.
5. Blue is everywhere in this category, so a blue-leaning teal keeps the trust and still stands apart.

Why v2 felt pale: every colour was pulled toward beige, so nothing stood out and the hierarchy was flat. v3 brings back saturation, but each colour gets one job.

THE PALETTE - 6 COLOURS, IN ORDER OF IMPORTANCE

1. LAGOON TEAL #0B7A74 (primary action)
   Job: everything you tap to move forward. Primary buttons, the active tab, toggles, links, selected progress.
   Tint for soft fills: #E2F2F0
   White text on it: 5.2:1, passes accessibility for body text.
   Why: blue-green signals trust and calm (like Kinedu and Headspace) and sets us apart from the all-blue competitors.

2. SUNSET CORAL #FF7A59 (energy / featured)
   Job: the one thing on each screen we most want tapped. Today's activity card, the onboarding hero, the "+ new" badge, the stop button while recording.
   Tint: #FFE8E1
   Use dark Night Plum text on it (5.8:1). Never put white body text on coral.
   Why: the warm, emotional colour. It brings the Lingokids energy without the shouting.

3. MARIGOLD #FFB937 (reward)
   Job: only for things you have earned. Golden leaves, the leaf counter, celebrations, streaks, "Done today".
   Tint: #FFF3D9
   Why: borrowed from games. If gold always means "you earned this", parents learn to love seeing it.

4. DREAM LAVENDER #6A5AE0 (Mira only)
   Job: anything Mira says or does. Her pearl, "Mira's read", the answer tabs, the voice waveform, the thinking state.
   Tint: #EEEBFF
   White text on it: 5.0:1.
   Why: gives the AI its own recognisable colour, gentle and slightly magical, so parents always know when Mira is speaking.

5. NIGHT PLUM #2A2340 (ink)
   Job: headings and body text (secondary text at 65%), selected chips, the dark voice bar, night scenes.
   Why: softer and warmer than black, and gives strong contrast for readability.

6. OAT #FBF7F1 (base), with white #FFFFFF cards
   Job: page background, card surfaces, sheets.
   Sand #F1EBE2 is the one neutral for quiet fills like input fields and disabled buttons.
   Why: the warm off-white that Kinedu, Lingokids and Headspace all use. Calm, never clinical.

HIERARCHY AND HOW MUCH OF EACH

Proportions on a typical screen
- About 60%: Oat and white surfaces.
- About 25%: Night Plum text and icons.
- About 10%: Lagoon Teal actions.
- About 5%: the accents (Coral, Marigold, Lavender), each only where its job applies.

Rules that keep it consistent
- At most one Coral element per screen. It marks "start here".
- Marigold only ever means earned. Never use it for decoration or buttons.
- Lavender only ever means Mira. Nothing else in the app is purple.
- Teal is the only colour for primary buttons. Secondary buttons are white with a Night Plum outline.
- Never place two accent colours as large solid blocks side by side.
- Skill markers (EQ, SQ, IQ) are small tint chips with an icon, never big blocks:
  - Emotional (EQ): Coral tint.
  - Social (SQ): Teal tint.
  - Intelligence (IQ): Marigold tint.
- Illustrations draw from the same six colours plus their tints. The tree keeps its natural greens, and earned leaves are Marigold.

How it lands on key screens
- Home: Oat page, Coral "today's activity" card, white tree card with Marigold leaves, white read tiles, Teal tab highlight.
- Ask Mira: Oat page, Lavender pearl and waveform, white answer cards with Lavender tabs, Teal send button.
- Activity: Coral header art, white step card, Teal "Next", Marigold celebration.
- Journey: white stat cards (the leaves count in Marigold), Teal progress bars, Night Plum for today's day in the week strip.
